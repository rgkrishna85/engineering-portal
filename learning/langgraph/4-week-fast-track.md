# 4-week plan, full content (all 32 sessions, 16 hours)

The full 7-week course, delivered in **4 weeks**. Nothing is cut. Every session is still a normal 30-minute block with the usual format, quiz and scoring (see the [README](README.md#4-how-every-30-minute-session-runs)). The only change is that **12 days have two sessions instead of one**.

| Week | Days | Daily time | Hours that week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 double days + 1 single | 60 / 30 min | 4.5 h |
| 2 | 4 double days + 1 single | 60 / 30 min | 4.5 h |
| 3 | 4 double days + 1 single | 60 / 30 min | 4.5 h |
| 4 | 5 single days (capstone) | 30 min (+ optional 30 min catch-up) | 2.5 h |
| **Total** | 20 days, 32 sessions | | **16 h** |

## How to run a double day
- **Best:** one block in the morning and one in the afternoon. The gap helps the first topic sink in, and the afternoon warm-up checks it.
- **If it must be back-to-back:** take a 5-minute break between the blocks, and don't skip the warm-up of block 2.
- **Rotate roles every block, not every day**, so everyone drives about equally.
- **Score each block separately** (/10). The tracker rows stay the same.
- Milestones and capstone days are always **single** sessions, so nobody is rushed on the days that are scored /20.

## Watch-outs at this pace
- **Prepare the day before:** the facilitator runs that day's code files once beforehand. On a double day that means two lessons to prepare.
- **🟡/🔴 scores pile up faster.** Use the warm-up of the *next block* (not the next day) to revisit them, and the optional capstone-week blocks as a buffer.
- **Weeks 1–3 are the heavy ones** (4.5 h each). If a day gets lost, move its second block into the week-4 optional slots instead of skipping it.
